## Repository guide

**Purpose:** A curated place for investment education, references, and related research materials rather than a verified portfolio-management application.

**Suggested organization:** For each resource, record the publisher, access date, asset class, topic, and whether the information is educational, historical, or regulatory. Verify external links and distinguish published facts from opinions.

**Disclaimer:** Resources are informational and do not constitute individualized investment advice.
